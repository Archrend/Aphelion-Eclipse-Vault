**Species:** Transformed [[Arcanid]]  

**Faction:** [[The Cultists]]

**Rank:**  Sage

**Purpose:** _Same as Rank_

**Current Location:** [[The Outer Wilds]]

**Encounter Stage:** Level 1

**Danger Level:**  Major Threat 


Among the outer ruins of the fracture, a vile creature known only as the Abyssal Weaver is said to be an echo of [[Xel'erim]]'s once-mighty form. 

The simulacrum is an abomination, born from the dark essence of the forgotten tomes, and it is believed to have absorbed a minuscule fragment of [[Xel'erim]]'s lingering power from a tome collected nearby, twisting it into something unholy and monstrous.

This lesser echo of the Profaned Protector roams the outer ruins, now searching for lost knowledge, upholding what remains of its  [[Xel'erim]]'s tragic legacy. Some believe [[Xel'erim]] lives on deep within [[The Lower Archives]], her eternal thirst for knowledge unfulfilled. But whether the creature is truly  [[Xel'erim]]  or a new abomination formed from her essence, it is a thing best left undisturbed.
