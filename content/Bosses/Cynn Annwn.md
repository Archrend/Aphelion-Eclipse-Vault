Once a noble protector, [[Cynn Annwn]] stood watch over the boundary between the mortal realm and the abyss. She was a fierce [[Hethrûn]], her very being a shield against the horrors that lurked beyond [[The Veil]]. But when [[The Fracture]] occurred, the rift between worlds tore apart the fabric of reality, twisting her purpose and corrupting her mind. [[The Abyss]] she once fought against consumed her, and the protector became the predator.

Unlike the other [[Hethrûn]] in [[The Realm]], whose bonds with their Watchers were retained during [[The Fracture]], [[Cynn Annwn]]'s Watcher sacrificed his soul to protect her. This act allowed her to regain a sense of her former self and re-enter [[The Veil]] unlike the other [[Hethrûn]] in [[The Realm]], who were still bound to their Watchers fates. For a time, she retained more of her sanity, a fleeting moment of clarity in the overwhelming darkness of [[The Abyss]].

However, the corruption of her mind was inevitable. The forces that twisted the fabric of reality had seeped deep into her mind, and no amount of strength could fully shield her from them. Despite her Watcher's sacrifice, [[Cynn Annwn]] could not resist the pull of [[The Abyss]]. Slowly, over time, she began to give into the darkness, her mind slipping further into madness. The fragment of her Watcher’s soul, though it had restored her, could not keep the corruption at bay.

The imbalance within her soul, ensured she would be unable to stay in [[The Veil]] for extended periods of time, as her very presence made her a target for other non-corrupted [[Hethrûn]] who still guard the non-shattered parts of  [[The Veil]]. Their sacred duty as protectors and arbiters of [[The Veil]] demanded they hunt her down, for the corruption in her soul threatened the delicate balance between realms. She was no longer the pure protector she once was, and the [[Hethrûn]] who remained loyal to their sacred oaths sought to rid [[The Veil]] of her twisted presence.

Driven out of [[The Veil]] by those who once stood alongside her, [[Cynn Annwn]] is forced to periodically return to the mortal realm, where she now roams with unmatched power. Her watchers sacrifice has made her a formidable being, able to slip between both [[The Veil]] and world at will. But her power comes at a great cost. The darkness she fought against now resides within her, and though she has grown far stronger than any [[Hethrûn]] was meant to be, she is a creature of imbalance, hunted in [[The Veil]] by those she once called allies.

She is a terrifying paradox, a being of immense power and eternal hunger. Though she is still plagued by the corruption that has consumed her, she now walks the line between [[The Veil]]and the world of the living, capable of shifting between both realms with ease. She hunts those who dare to attempt cross the boundary, her purpose intact and forever tied to [[The Veil]]. The once-noble protector is now a relentless force, a predator with the power to destroy not just the living, but the very fabric of reality itself.

Her once-pristine fur is now blackened by the abyss, her eyes burning with the cold, empty light of forgotten stars. Blinded by the very darkness she once fought against, [[Cynn Annwn]] is a creature of pure madness and hunger. But within her, the faint echoes of the Watcher's sacrifice remain, a reminder of what she once was, even as she plunges deeper into the abyss.
