The Fallen Guardian once stood at the great gates of the royal city, an immense construct built to deny passage to those unworthy. It watched over the kingdom for decades, unwavering and unbreakable. During [[The Fracture]], a surge of abyssal energy struck the Guardian, warping its once-complete arcane runes and twisting the magic that gave it life. Its glowing core dimmed, flickering with unstable power, and its once-precise movements became sluggish and erratic. 

With no city left to protect, it wandered aimlessly, striking down anything that came too close. For years, it roamed the broken roads, attacking travelers and lost souls alike, unable to understand that the world it once knew was gone. Eventually, its cracked frame could carry it no further. It collapsed at the outer edge of the fracture, its massive body half-buried in rubble, vines creeping through its shattered limbs. Its core still pulsed weakly, but it no longer had the strength to move. Now, as the abyss spreads once again, the Guardian stirs. It is a shell of what it once was, its power nearly spent, but its last command still lingers in what remains of its mind—none shall pass.
