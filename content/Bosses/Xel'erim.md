Xel'erim, once a promising scholar of the [[Arcanid]] race, began her life with an unquenchable thirst for forbidden knowledge. Born a lesser [[Arcanid]], denoted as Xel'eb, she was destined to remain in the shadows of [[The Upper Archives]]. Yet, through cunning and ruthless intellect, Xel'erim quickly ascended the ranks, defying her status as a lowborn scholar. She grew obsessed with the mysteries hidden within [[The Lower Archives]], a place veiled in secrecy and forbidden to most of her kind. It was within these crumbling halls that she found her true purpose: to ascend beyond the limitations of her station. 

When the current protector of the archives, Cel'erim, neared the end of her existence, a traditional duel to the death was held between three higher-ranked [[Arcanid]] — Xal'ilk, Cel'ilk, and Tel'ilk — to determine who would assume the mantle of Protector. Xel'erim, though still a lesser [[Arcanid]] at the time, was the daughter of Xal'ilk, and when her mother was slain in the brutal contest, [[Xel'erim]]'s sharp mind and many alliances led to her swift rise as the next Broodmother. 

A day before Cel'ilk was the claim the mantle, Xel'erim challenged Cel'ilk to a duel. The combat was brutal, but Xel'erim emerged victorious, seizing the title of Protector of the Archives. However, with this newfound power came a growing, insatiable hunger. The knowledge of the archives was vast, yet still not enough to satiate her desires. She began to secretly devour the minds and bodies of the brightest students, absorbing their knowledge and their very essence in a twisted attempt to reach the upper echelons of knowledge, knowledge far beyond what her [[Arcanid]] kin could ever possess. 

In time, the transformation of Xel'erim became irreversible. The Broodmother’s body grew grotesquely large, unable to leave [[The Lower Archives]], as her final brood of malformed children hatched. These ascended offspring were grotesque protectors, monstrous in form, bound by the dark essence of their mother’s will. 

Yet, with the arrival of [[The Fracture]], an event that shattered the archives and tore apart the very foundation of [[The Realm]], [[Xel'erim]]'s fate became uncertain. The Protector's body was never found, and rumors whispered that her essence had not perished, but lingered in the depths, twisted into something unrecognizable. Those who dared venture into [[The Upper Archives]] claimed that [[Xel'erim]]'ss power still lingered in the tomes scattered across the desolate, collapsed ruins.
