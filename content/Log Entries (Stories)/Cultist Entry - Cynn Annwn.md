
The trees… they whisper. 

A century since [[The Fracture]], and [[The Realm]] is a nightmare of twisted shadows. 

I thought I could transcend, control [[The Abyss]], become something more. But now I’m running, and I’m the prey. 

She is real. The one who stalks the shattered veil. The hunter. She stalks those like me, those who reach too far into [[The Abyss]] for power. I thought I could escape her, but I was wrong.

The land around me shifts. The trees bend like claws reaching for my flesh, the ground warps underfoot like it's alive. And I feel her, a presence, cold and endless, like the moonless night. Her hunger is palpable, her eyes unseen, but always watching. She is the wolf, moving between the cracks of reality, and I am the prey. 

I hear the soft padding of paws in the distance, low growls that vibrate through the earth. She moves with patience, a predator who knows time is nothing. Every step is deliberate, calculated. The air thickens, choking me with the pressure of her approach. 

I can’t outrun her. I can’t hide. Her breath is cold on my neck, a whisper in the dark. I feel the brush of her shadow, her fangs snapping closer with each frantic step I take. 

I hear the howl, a cry of hunger that shakes the very trees, an echo of [[The Abyss]] itself. It draws closer, and with it, I feel my mind unravel. She is not just hunting my body. She is hunting my sanity, waiting for me to crack, to fall into the dark where she can feast.

The woods are closing in. The air is growing heavy. I am losing my grip. Her growls are a constant hum in my ears, relentless.

I can't breathe. 

I can hear her, too close now. Her breath, sharp and chilling. Her fangs, just a step behind.

And then, the soft rhythmic pattering of her paws fades, replaced by a deafening silence.

The chase is over.
