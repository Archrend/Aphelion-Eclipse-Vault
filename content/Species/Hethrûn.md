The [[Hethrûn]] are a rare and ancient species of celestial hounds, known for their ethereal presence, unmatched strength, and profound connection to the cosmic order. These majestic creatures were once companions to the exalted ranks of the [[Watchers of the Veil]], a prestigious order tasked with guarding the delicate balance between realms. The [[Hethrûn]] were not merely symbols of power, they were living embodiments of the sacred duties held by the watchers, beings whose very presence in the world was a mark of prestige and reverence.

These creatures are known for their shimmering fur that glowed with celestial light and their haunting, piercing eyes that could see through the veil of reality. The _Hethrûn_ are not only protectors but also trackers, able to sense disturbances in the cosmic balance with unerring precision. Few in number, they were seen only in the presence of the most revered Watchers, their companionship a mark of extraordinary status. The [[Hethrûn]] are feared, respected, and sometimes even seen as omens, for their presence outside of the royal palace often foretold great events or dark times to come.

However, becoming the companion of a [[Hethrûn]] was not earned lightly. It required passing the [[Ritual of Shattering]], a dangerous and near-impossible trial. The ritual primarily involved tearing and entering a small fracture in the veil, alerting a nearby [[Hethrûn]].  None survive the [[Ritual of Shattering]], as the ritual was not about defeating the [[Hethrûn]], for that was impossible, but rather about proving worthiness through a self-sacrificial battle.

To face a [[Hethrûn]] within the veil was to face death itself. A Watcher seeking this eternal bond had to prove their worth through a trial of combat with the [[Hethrûn]]. If the Watcher performed well enough in battle, demonstrating skill, valor, and a deep understanding of their sacred duties, the [[Hethrûn]] would tear a fragment from its own soul, sacrificing its freedom and duty within [[The Veil]] to restore the Watcher to life. In doing so, the bond between the [[Hethrûn]] and the Watcher would be forged for eternity.

If a Watcher failed to prove themselves worthy, if they were deemed lacking in strength, courage, or conviction the [[Hethrûn]] would rend soul from flesh. Their soul lost forever, cast into [[The Veil]] a fate worse than death, condemned to drift forever in the veil where no light or hope could reach. The soul would never return, its essence lost to the endless space between reality.

To attempt to bind with a [[Hethrûn]] was to accept death. Those who sought to condemn others to death within the veil had to first face it themselves and survive. For those who succeeded, the price was steep. The bond was eternal, and the Watcher would gain immortality, but at a heavy cost. By binding to a [[Hethrûn]], they accepted its duty, forever bound to the hunt for any being that attempts to slip past the veil. Their connection to the [[Hethrûn]] made them more than mortal, but in this attempt, they lost their freedom and humanity. While their personalities often remained, their duty bound them to commit acts that many would deem immoral. Their existence was forever tied to the forces beyond, and they could never return to the life they once knew.

The [[Hethrûn]] were not merely companions, they were the gatekeepers of life and death, the force that stood between [[The Realm]] and [[The Abyss]]. And their legacy endured, even after [[The Fracture]], when the [[Hethrûn]] were twisted into nightmarish forms, their original bond with the Watchers eternal but corrupted. But despite this, the [[Hethrûn]] remain a testament to the price of defying death and the consequences of disturbing the natural order.
