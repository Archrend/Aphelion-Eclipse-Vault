
**Purpose:** High Court Executioner 
**Danger Level:** Extreme Threat

The Justiciars were once the silent hand of the kingdom’s highest court, built solely for delivering final judgment. They would execute traitors and criminals with a single, precise swing of their enchanted blades. Now, corrupted by the abyss, they see all as guilty. Their hands glow with searing arcane brands, marking enemies for destruction, and their enchanted blades remain sharper than steel, cutting through even the strongest defenses. Travelers unlucky enough to cross their path never live to tell the tale.
