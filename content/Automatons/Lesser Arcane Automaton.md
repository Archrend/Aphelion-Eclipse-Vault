**Purpose:** Farmhand & Builder Assistant 

**Danger Level:** Minor Threat 

Once designed to aid in farming and construction, these simple automatons now wander aimlessly, their cores flickering weakly as they attempt to carry out forgotten tasks. Most are harmless unless provoked, their rusted limbs too slow to pose a real threat. However, when disturbed, they lash out with surprising strength, mistaking travelers for obstacles in their non-existent labor.
