**Purpose:** Palace & Noble Protection

**Danger Level:** Severe Threat 

These elite automatons once guarded the royal chambers, entrusted with the safety of nobles and high-ranking officials. Now, they stalk the ruins of the lower city, their armor blackened by abyssal corruption. When they detect a threat, they emit a piercing shriek, once a signal to summon other [[Royal Warden]]s and [[Fractured Enforcer]], now a chilling omen for any traveler within earshot.
