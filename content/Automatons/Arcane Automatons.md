Forged in a time of order and progress, arcane automatons once served as the lifeblood of the kingdom’s labor and defense. These constructs, powered by intricate magical cores, were built to assist in everything from farming and construction to policing the streets. Their towering forms, crafted from stone and enchanted metals, carried out their duties with unwavering precision. The royal and military models, designed for battle, stood as symbols of authority, ensuring the peace within the city walls.

But when [[The Fracture]] tore through the land, the very magic that sustained them became their undoing. The abyssal surge shattered many where they stood, their cores burning out in a final, blinding flash. Others, however, were twisted by the corruption, their once-purposeful actions turning mindless and violent. The most powerful of them—the royal guardians and city enforcers—were mostly destroyed in the chaos, but some managed to stagger free, wandering into the wilderness beyond the ruins. Now, these once-loyal constructs roam the land without direction, attacking anything that strays too close, unable to tell friend from foe.

With [[The Abyss]] growing stronger, long-dormant automatons are beginning to stir. The fractured lands are littered with their broken forms, buried beneath dust and decay, waiting. Some believe [[The Abyss]] is reawakening them, their cores now pulsing with something far darker than the magic that once gave them life. To travelers and scavengers alike, these constructs are no longer tools of order, but harbingers of destruction—silent, relentless, and lost to time.
